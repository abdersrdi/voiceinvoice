# Talkstamp Privacy Policy

_Draft for review before App Store submission. Last updated: 2026-11-10._

Talkstamp is designed to keep your business data on your own devices.

## What the app stores
- **Invoices, customers, shop profile, stamp and signature** are stored on your device and synced through **your own iCloud account** (Apple's CloudKit private database). The developer cannot see this data and runs no servers for it.
- **Voice profiles (voiceprints):** if you enroll employee voices, only a numeric voiceprint is stored, never the enrollment audio. Voiceprints sync through the shop's iCloud account unless the employee opts out. Each person can delete their voiceprint at any time, which removes it from all devices. Enrollment requires the person's explicit consent.

## Speech recognition
Dictation uses Apple's Speech framework. When your device and language support it, recognition runs **on the device**. Otherwise audio may be sent to Apple for processing under Apple's privacy terms. The dictation screen shows which mode is active. Talkstamp does not store recordings.

## Camera and microphone
The camera is used only to scan your company stamp. The microphone is used only while you dictate.

## Purchases
Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Talkstamp does not receive your payment details.

## What we do not do
No advertising, no analytics trackers, no selling or sharing of your data.

## Your choices
You can delete invoices and profiles in the app, delete voiceprints, and remove all iCloud data from your Apple ID settings.
