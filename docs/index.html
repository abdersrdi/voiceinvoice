Talkstamp Support

Talkstamp turns what you say into a finished PDF invoice with your company stamp and signature.

Get help

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue: github.com/abdersrdi/voiceinvoice/issues. Please do not post customer data or invoices.

Common questions

Dictation does not start. Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition in Settings, Talkstamp.

Cancel my subscription. Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Talkstamp Pro.

Restore a purchase. Open the subscription screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases.
